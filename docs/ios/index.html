Muses / iPhone & iPad

Your collection, on the move.

Erato brings Muses to iPhone and iPad. Explore videos and playlists, keep a personal collection, and return to the moments you want to remember.

Keep your collection close

  • Search and browse videos, channels and playlists.
  • Save favorites, organize playlists and revisit your history.
  • Choose Music or Video layouts and manage your playback queue.
  • Write notes and save time bookmarks alongside a video.
  • Enjoy layouts that adapt to iPhone and iPad.

A beta for your device

Requires iOS 18 or later. Erato is currently in beta, with separate Public and experimental Native editions. The Public edition pauses video playback when the player closes or the app enters the background. Experimental background audio still requires further validation.

Installation depends on the edition's signing and device requirements. Read the release notes and installation guide before choosing a version.
