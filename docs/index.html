Discover from Home

Artwork leads the way, while the floating player keeps your current song close.

Captured macOS app screens from the published tour; these do not represent iOS or Windows, or every subsequent development change. This tour switches views; playback and account actions happen in the Mac app.

01 / DISCOVER

Bring your YouTube library along.

Import playlists, browse music, albums, artists and videos, and keep favorites and listening history together.

02 / LISTEN

A queue with context.

Previous, Next, Up Next, repeat and shuffle work with the collection you started from. Control playback from the app, menu bar, keyboard or system media keys.

03 / GO DEEPER

More than a cover.

Open Now Playing for artwork and available lyrics, switch to vinyl, watch the video on demand, or return to a podcast where you left off.
