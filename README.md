<h1 align="center">Project Muses</h1>
<p align="center">One music home. Three platforms.</p>

**Muses** is a family of native music applications for Mac, iPhone, iPad and Windows. It brings artwork, collections and playback together in a focused space, so you can spend more time with the music you love.

Polyhymnia on macOS is the heart of the project. Erato and Euterpe carry the same product direction to iOS and Windows, with an experience shaped for each device.

## A place for your music

- **Explore by artwork.** Browse songs, albums, artists and playlists in a clear, visual library.
- **Keep your collection close.** Organize playlists, save favorites and revisit your listening history.
- **Stay in the flow.** Move between browsing, the player and your queue without losing your place.
- **Make room for the song.** Enjoy an expressive Now Playing view, artwork and available lyrics.

Features and availability vary by platform.

## The Muses family

| Project | Platform | Experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Polyhymnia](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Muses-Polyhymnia) | macOS 14+ | The core desktop experience, with an immersive player and native Mac controls |
| [Erato](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Muses-Erato) | iOS 18+ · iPhone & iPad | A portable collection with adaptive layouts, notes and time bookmarks; currently in beta |
| [Euterpe](https://github.com/xiaotwu/Muses-Euterpe) | Windows 11 | A desktop adaptation with media controls and tray integration; currently in development |
